Everyone Can Use German

A long-term approach with a six-month starting phase—for newcomers to the DACH region and for anyone who is no longer young but still wants to learn German. Read it online chapter by chapter or download the complete PDF for free.

Do Not Just Learn German—Live in German Author: Yijian He · First draft v0.1 · 6 August 2026

Inspired by the methodology of Li Xiaolai’s Everyone Can Use English, this book transfers the central idea of “use” to German: daily tasks, conversations, reading German books and the Bible, and job interviews all become real German practice. The 63-page book also covers information bubbles, a daily task system, and a three-stage six-month path.
